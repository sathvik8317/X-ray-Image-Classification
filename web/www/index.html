Research demo, not a medical device. Trained on pediatric X-rays from one hospital (Kaggle Chest X-Ray Pneumonia). On its test set: 97.9% sensitivity, 76.9% specificity, AUC 0.974. It over-calls pneumonia and shows signs of shortcut learning. Red areas in the heatmap pushed the model toward its answer. Code and results
